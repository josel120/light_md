light_md 1.4 is the biggest update yet: tabs, spell checking, an outline, a distraction-free mode, exports to HTML and Word, a Windows installer, and an interface in five languages.

## New

- **Tabs**: keep several documents open (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+Tab). Each has its own undo history, and they all come back the next time you start light_md.
- **Spell checking** in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German. Misspelled words get a red wave; right-click for corrections or to add a word to your dictionary. Choose the language in **Options → Spelling**.
- **Outline** on the left: every title and subtitle. Click one to jump to it in both panes. Ctrl+Shift+O hides it.
- **Distraction-free mode** (F11): full screen with only your text, in a comfortable column. Esc brings everything back.
- **Export to HTML** (one page with the pictures inside) and **to .odt** for Word, LibreOffice and Google Docs. Pictures from outside the document's folder are only included if you say yes.
- **Paste pictures** with Ctrl+V: a screenshot is saved next to your document and shows up in the preview.
- **Syntax highlighting** in the writing pane: bigger titles, bold, italic, links and code stand out, in your theme's colors.
- **Interface in English, Español, Português, Français and Deutsch**. It follows your system; change it in **Options → Language**.
- **Never lose your work**: light_md keeps a copy of unsaved changes and offers it back after a crash.
- **Synchronized scrolling**, a **Dark theme** for every system, **bigger or smaller text** (Ctrl + mouse wheel), and light_md **reopens your document where you left off**.

## Windows

- New **installer** (`windows-x64-setup.exe`): Start menu entry, uninstaller, and light_md offered in "Open with" for `.md` files. No administrator rights needed. The zip is still there if you prefer it.
- If you used the installer, **Check for updates** offers the next installer.

## Downloads

- **Windows**: `light_md-v1.4.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` (installer) or `light_md-v1.4.0-windows-x64.zip` (unzip and open `bin\light_md.exe`).
- **macOS** 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel: `light_md-v1.4.0-macos.zip`. The app isn't signed by Apple yet: the first time, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Linux**: build from source or use `packaging/arch/PKGBUILD` (see the README).
